Data Usage

AI Church Broadcast may use microphone access to capture live speech for sermon notes and may use internet access for scripture lookup, connected integrations, updates, and related broadcast features.

Locally stored information can include session details, account details, notes, planner progress, and archive history on the device or browser used by the user.

Advertising

The web app may display third-party advertisements. Advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device information, or similar technologies to serve, measure, and improve ads where permitted by law and user consent.

Users can manage advertising and cookie choices through browser settings and any consent controls made available on the website.

User Rights

  • Users may clear locally stored data by logging out, removing browser storage, or deleting app data on their device.
  • Users may choose not to use connected features that require internet access.
