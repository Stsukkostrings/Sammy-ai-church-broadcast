About The App

Empowering organizations through intelligent communication and media automation.

OmniCast AI transforms live speech into structured, useful content in real time. It helps teams capture spoken sessions, generate notes and summaries, prepare overlays, record audio, and manage content for distribution across digital channels.

What It Does

  • Continuous mic listening in supported browsers
  • Editable live session notes with download and share
  • Session audio recording with download support
  • Livestream overlay and lower-third preparation
  • Session archive for reusable history
  • Planner for live production execution
  • AI-ready summaries and promo snippets
  • Scripture assistance and faith-study tools where needed
  • Worship lyric auto-detection for church workflows

Who It Serves

  • Church and faith media teams
  • Corporate communications departments
  • Schools, universities, and training events
  • NGOs and advocacy organizations
  • Government institutions and public teams
  • Conference and event production crews
  • Studios, livestream teams, and media desks
